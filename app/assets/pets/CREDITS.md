## The `hero` pack — original work

The sixteen heroes in `app/assets/pets/hero/` are **not** third-party art. They
are generated from scratch by `app/tools/make_heroes.py`: a shared chibi body rig
posed frame by frame, plus a palette and a few silhouette switches (cape, mask
style, build) per character. Nothing is traced from, derived from, or based on
any existing character, so they carry no third-party licence at all — edit the

## The `warrior` pack — original work

The six fighters in `app/assets/pets/warrior/` are likewise **not** third-party
art. `app/tools/make_warriors.py` generates them on the same rig as the heroes —
it imports the frame poses, the outliner and the GIF writer from
`make_heroes.py` rather than copying them — and adds the equipment that is
actually what tells these apart at the size they are seen: helm, shield, weapon
and skirt. No two share a helm. Nothing is traced from, derived from or based on
any existing character, so like the heroes they carry no third-party licence.

| | Palette | Helm | Shield | Weapon |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gladiator | bronze / gold | crested galea | round | short sword |
| centurion | red / gold | transverse crest | scutum | spear |
| knight | steel / blue | great helm | kite | sword |
| samurai | black lacquer / red | kabuto, crescent | — | katana |
| viking | leather / iron | horned, nasal bar | round | axe |
| pharaoh | blue / gold | nemes | — | crook |

They are drawn on a 40px-wide canvas rather than the heroes' 32, because a
figure with a shield out one side and a weapon out the other does not fit in
32 — at that width both were clipped into the torso and neither read as a
separate object. Canvas width costs nothing downstream: `gen-metrics.py`
measures the opaque box per clip and the renderer scales by that.

## The `fairy` pack — original work

Two figures in `app/assets/pets/fairy/`, generated by
`app/tools/make_fairies.py` on the same shared rig. This is the pack that
needed the body to grow parts the rig never had: a pointed-ear hood, a flared
tutu, a wand with a star on the end, and wings. The hood, the wand and the
wings are generic fairy-tale furniture -- there is no emblem, name or logo
taken from anywhere.

| | Palette | Wings |
|---|---|---|
| blossom | pink / pale pink | —, caped instead |
| nightshade | black / grey | —, caped instead |

Neither has wings, which makes `fairy` a poor name for what is left: an
eared hood, a cape, a tutu and a wand. The winged colour variants that
justified the name were cut. **The species id is worth renaming** if anything
else is ever added here -- it is the folder name and nothing else, but it is
also what the picker shows.

`draw_wings` is kept even though nothing calls it now. The `wings` flag
defaults to on, so a new entry gets them back by saying nothing; deleting the
routine would make that a rewrite rather than a one-line table edit.

The wings took three passes while they were in use, and the notes are worth
keeping for whoever switches them on again. Drawn small they clustered round
the torso as four indistinct blobs; drawn at full height they rose past the
ears and fell past the hem, and the figure stopped being a fairy with wings
and became a pair of wings with a face in the middle. They span the torso and
no more.

The cape went the same way in reverse. Hung down the back with wings present
it was invisible -- wings behind it, torso in front -- so it became a mantle
across the shoulders. With the wings gone there is room for it to hang again,
and both figures use it. `nightshade` keeps its legs a step lighter than its
cape: level with it, the two merged into a single dark trapezoid and the
figure lost its legs below the hem.

**On licensed characters:** sprite sets for Marvel, DC and similar properties
are not available under any licence that permits bundling them here — what
circulates online is unlicensed fan art, and this repository is public and MIT,
so shipping it would hand the problem on to everyone who cloned it. The `hero`
pack exists to scratch that itch honestly. `rose` is a caped, cowled hero in
pink, `crucible` is red-and-gold powered armour with a lit chest core, and
`cinnabar` is a red-and-blue masked acrobat with pale lenses, and `nocturne`
is a slate-and-black caped vigilante with an eared cowl and a bright belt --
the shapes people ask for most -- all drawn on our own rig, with no name,
emblem, logo, chest sigil or faceplate geometry borrowed from anyone.
`nocturne` carries no emblem at all, which is why the rig now lets one be
left off. If you own a sprite pack you are
entitled to use, the widget will pick it up with no code change: drop the frames
into `app/assets/pets/<name>/` named `<colour>_<action>_8fps.gif`, add an
`icon.png`, and run `python app/tools/gen-metrics.py`.
